About me

I'm Jean, a U.S. Navy veteran moving into cybersecurity after eight years as a lead line cook in Worcester. A kitchen runs on mise en place: everything prepared before the rush. I bring the same habit to security: tools configured, logs watched, procedures ready before an incident.

I'm building in public so you can see how I think, not just what I've studied. I work in English, Spanish and Portuguese, which helps when users and threats don't stick to one language.
